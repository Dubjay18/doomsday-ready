# 11 · Ideas to make it more fun

Ranked by fun ÷ effort. ★ = strongly recommended before Dec 18.

### Quick wins
1. ★ **"Readiness" personality on the verdict.** Below 50%: "Civilian. Please evacuate." 50–79%: "Sidekick. Close, but Doom would win." 80–94%: "READY." 95%+: "You ARE the multiverse." Each gets its own Ready Card art.
2. ★ **Speedrun-in-7-days challenge.** Start a timer when you pick Speedrun; finishing all 8 ticks + passing the exam in ≤ 7 days earns a gold card border.
3. ★ **Squad "assemble" meter.** A squad board shows how many members are READY ("4/7 assembled"). Opening night is more fun when the whole group chat is green.
4. **SFX generator.** Every correct answer picks a random onomatopoeia from a list (THWACK, KA-POW, BZZARK, SHRAKK…). Users can unlock rare ones.
5. **Variant you.** Avatar builder with mask colour, emblem and pattern; rare "variant" palettes unlock via achievements.
6. **Countdown easter eggs.** At 14:00:00 days-left etc., the hero changes copy; on Dec 17 the whole site switches to a "night before" dark theme.

### Medium
7. ★ **Daily Challenge streak calendar** styled as a comic strip, one panel per day you played.
8. **Recap in 60 seconds.** For optional titles, a swipeable 6-panel text recap ("Previously on…") so Crammers can skip and still pass.
9. **Hot-take polls** ("Who survives Doomsday?") with results revealed after the film. Pure engagement, no spoilers.
10. **"Who's in Doomsday" connection map.** Click a confirmed character → see which titles introduced them and whether you've watched those.
11. **Watch-party planner** for squads: pick dates, split the Speedrun across evenings, export .ics.
12. **Ready Card for WhatsApp/IG Stories** at 1080×1920 in addition to the 1200×630 OG card. In Nigeria especially, WhatsApp status is the main share surface.

### Bigger bets
13. **Versus duels.** Share a link; both players get the same seeded 10 questions; side-by-side result card.
14. **Boss Battle mode.** The Readiness Exam framed as a fight: each correct answer chips Doom's armour bar; three misses and you're "incurred". Same engine, different skin. Use an original villain silhouette rather than Doom's design.
15. **AI "Archivist" hint companion.** Instead of a static hint, a small LLM call (Anthropic TS SDK, server-side) generates a spoiler-safe nudge from the explanation, never the answer. Cap at 1 per exam.
16. **Post-film debrief.** After Dec 18, an opt-in spoiler quiz on Doomsday itself, which then feeds a "Secret Wars Ready" track for 2027.
17. **Creator/club mode.** Cinemas, campus clubs or influencers get a branded squad page and a QR code for opening-night queues.

### Guardrails for all of the above
- Keep everything original: no Marvel logos, character art or posters outside TMDB-licensed images.
- Spoilers: anything about *Brand New Day* or later sits behind spoiler-safe mode.
